AZMOR privacy policy

Who is responsible

Mouadh Ghabi operates AZMOR as an individual based in Tunisia. For privacy or account deletion, email contact@azmor.app; for product support, email support@azmor.app. We reply to emailed deletion requests within 30 days and verify ownership before deleting an account.

Account, training and nutrition

An account is required. Supabase processes your email, account identifier and authentication. Optional profile answers, workouts, program choices, nutrition, weight and account preferences support the features you choose and private account restoration. Logging saves locally first and works offline after first sign-in. Cloud requests require connectivity. Profile photos remain on your device; avatar choices can sync privately.

Adults only

AZMOR is intended for adults aged 18 and over. First-run setup requires an explicit local 18+ self-declaration; a supplied birthday or age indicating under 18 stops setup until reviewed or explicitly corrected. Birthday is optional. This is self-declaration, not independent age verification. The declaration itself is local; optional birthday/profile facts can sync privately.

Health Connect, steps and weight

Optional Health Connect access reads steps, and distance or active calories when already granted. New activity permission requests are limited to steps. Separate optional weight exchange imports measurements and writes your explicit weigh-ins to Health Connect. Activity is stored as daily/hourly aggregates. Phone step counting can use Android physical activity access. Manual steps remain local. Disabling access stops future reads; it does not automatically remove facts already saved. Activity calories do not increase the nutrition budget.

Coach through Google Gemini

When you submit a Coach request, your question and permitted context pass through the AZMOR backend to Google Gemini. Backend consent and your device restrictions jointly control Training, Nutrition, Weight, Activity, XP and Profile. Automatic Profile sharing starts off. With Profile enabled, context can contain first name, derived age, sex, height and selected training/food preferences; exact birthday, full name, email, account ID and free notes are excluded from automatic context. Text you type can still contain sensitive information. Only explicitly confirmed preferences become memory. Health Connect-derived facts may be included only under their allowed scopes and explicit consent to this purpose. Google Gemini is an external processor for the inputs you choose to submit. Its handling follows the applicable Gemini API terms and Google privacy policy. AZMOR does not control Google’s independent security, legal or infrastructure retention and does not promise zero retention outside its systems.

Food photos and voice

Explicit meal analysis sends selected text or a resized metadata-free food photo through AZMOR to Google Gemini. AI food names, portions, nutrient estimates and assumptions remain editable before you confirm a diary entry. Supported voice recognition runs on-device; no raw audio is sent by AZMOR. Recognized text can be sent when you request analysis.

Private body progress photos

Body photos and thumbnails are encrypted with device Keystore AES-GCM in private app storage. Their date, pose, optional note, recorded weight and comparison reports remain local. They are excluded from account sync, cloud backup and device transfer and do not move to a new phone. Capture and browsing work offline. Only pressing Analyze after the separate body-photo disclosure sends the two chosen metadata-free images through AZMOR to Google Gemini, together with weight/session facts separately permitted by your Coach scopes. Photo notes and exact birthday are excluded. AZMOR does not save the images or comparison results in server chat, database or application logs; provider and infrastructure processing/retention are separate. Single-photo deletion removes its pixels, local record and related comparison reports.

Reporting, notifications and sharing

The Report action on a server-generated Coach reply sends its identifiers, reason and optional note (up to 500 characters) to AZMOR/Supabase for safety review, without sending the report to Gemini. Offline reports are queued; only a server acknowledgement means sent. Optional workout, weight, birthday and progress-photo reminders are scheduled on your device under notification settings. Explicit image/file sharing gives the selected file to your chosen app; its policies then apply. Community is hidden for launch; it is not a public social feature in this release. No advertising, advertising-ID collection or active analytics/crash SDK is added. Android vitals is operated by Google Play.

Other processors and this website

Supabase handles cloud authentication and private records; Cloudflare can route protected food search/barcode requests. An enabled Open Food Facts barcode fallback sends the barcode and public app identification, without your AZMOR bearer, profile or diary. Google receives requested AI inputs. Providers can process IP addresses and request/security metadata. The GitHub Pages host logs visitor IP addresses for security under GitHub’s privacy policy. This site has no advertising, analytics cookies or third-party scripts. Its deletion form uses your email and a one-time code with Supabase. Login tokens stay in memory; an account identifier, random operation ID and recovery proof are stored in this browser until deletion is confirmed.

Retention

Saved account records remain until you delete them or the account, subject to the recovery boundaries below. Saved Coach conversations remain until you delete the conversation, clear Coach history or delete the account. The device keeps a bounded offline cache; older saved conversations remain available through the server. Keeping history does not send your full history to Google: provider context stays bounded to the existing recent six turns and permitted facts. Confirmed constraints still expire after 30 days, preferences/style after 90 days, and replay/decision receipts after 90 days; working-turn and result lifetimes remain separate. Expiry cleanup can be lazy on account access and does not promise physical erasure at that exact time. Temporary meal-photo retry eligibility is 10 minutes, editable meal drafts 24 hours and server meal results have a 1-hour logical expiry. Local confirmed correction receipts and body comparison reports remain until their applicable deletion/account-local wipe. Coach reports and the identifiers-only answer registry remain until account deletion, even after chat deletion.

AZMOR’s content-free operational infrastructure logs are retained for no more than 30 days. Any database backup created and controlled by AZMOR is rotated or deleted within 30 days. This does not promise that automatic backups exist or that deleted records can be restored. Independently managed provider logs and backups follow the provider’s applicable terms and retention.

Account deletion and remaining copies

Use the in-app deletion controls or the web deletion form. The existing prepare/finalize protocol purges owned cloud records before deleting the authentication identity. In-app deletion also verifies removal of local app data. Web deletion cannot remotely erase an offline phone, private progress photos or files already exported to someone else. The app learns account deletion when it next contacts the service; manage any retained device copies explicitly. A minimal proof-hash recovery receipt and redacted tombstone remain while recovery is possible; no finite receipt retention/abandoned-operation cleanup policy is implemented. Provider backups/logs and processing have separate retention. We reply to requests sent to contact@azmor.app within 30 days. This response period is not a promise of simultaneous erasure of every provider or offline-device copy. AZMOR-controlled content-free logs and any AZMOR-created database backups have a maximum retention of 30 days; provider-managed copies and the disclosed deletion-recovery receipt have separate boundaries.

Your choices and security

You can refuse optional permissions, edit facts, restrict Coach categories, delete history/memory separately, export your data and request account deletion. App and provider endpoints use HTTPS; private device storage and encrypted progress photos do not imply end-to-end encryption of cloud/AI data. Ordinary sign-out preserves existing local data. For access, correction, export, deletion or privacy questions, contact contact@azmor.app. We verify ownership before disclosing or deleting account data and process requests under applicable law. External processors may handle data in countries other than yours under their own applicable contractual and legal safeguards; AZMOR does not promise control over their independent systems.
